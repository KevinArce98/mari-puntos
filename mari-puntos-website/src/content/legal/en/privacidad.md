## 1. Introduction

Welcome to MariPuntos. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use and protect your personal information when you use our mobile app.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Account Information

- Name
- Email address
- Profile information

### 2.2 Usage Data

- Completed actions
- Requested and approved permissions
- Points earned and current level
- Unlocked achievements
- Weekly streaks

### 2.3 Technical Information

- Device type
- Operating system
- Unique device identifiers
- App usage data
- Error and crash reports
- A technical recording of the on-screen session when an error occurs, with all text and images masked (real content is never captured)

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

- Provide and maintain our service
- Manage your account and relationship with your partner
- Calculate points and levels
- Improve the user experience
- Communicate with you about updates
- Ensure the security of the app

## 4. Sharing Information

MariPuntos only shares information:

- With your linked partner inside the app
- When required by law
- With trusted service providers (authentication, storage)

## 5. Data Security

We implement technical and organizational security measures to protect your data:

- Encryption of data in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
- Secure authentication via Clerk
- Secure storage of credentials
- Restricted access to personal data

## 6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

- Access your personal data
- Correct inaccurate information
- Delete your account and data
- Export your data
- Withdraw consent

## 7. Data Retention

We keep your personal information while:

- Your account is active
- It is necessary to provide services
- It is required by legal obligations

## 8. Third-Party Services

MariPuntos uses the following third-party services:

- **Clerk**: User authentication
- **Sentry**: Error and crash monitoring to improve app stability, including a technical screen recording with text and images masked when an error occurs
- **Our own backend**: Storage of app data

## 9. Minors

MariPuntos is designed for users over 18 years old. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through:

- An in-app notification
- Email
